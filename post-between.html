The week is part of the work

A short read from the practice · 3 min

An hour is a small slice of a week. People sometimes leave a session feeling clear, then meet a Tuesday that does not care about their clarity. They come back apologizing, as if the ordinary week were a test they failed.

It is not a test. The days between sessions are where the work either becomes livable or stays as an idea. That is why the practice here favors one small thing to notice, not a pile of homework. Notice when your shoulders rise before a certain conversation. Notice the hour you disappear into your phone. Notice the sentence you almost said.

Noticing is different from fixing. Fixing asks you to be a project. Noticing asks you to be present for a moment you would usually rush past. Most change starts there, and it is allowed to be uneven. A week can go sideways. You can forget the thing you meant to try. You can come back and say so. That, too, is information.

If the space between sessions feels too wide, say that. Pace is part of the care. Some seasons need a closer rhythm. Some need room. Neither one means you are doing therapy wrong.

These notes are reflections, not a plan for your particular life. If you are in crisis, call or text 988, or call 911.

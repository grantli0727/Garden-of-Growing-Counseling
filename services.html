01 — Individual

Individual Therapy

Find clarity and balance

A steady place to think out loud. People come for anxiety that will not quiet, a low mood that has settled in, grief, self-criticism, and the strain of holding everything together.

We go at your pace. Some hours are for understanding a pattern. Some are for finding one kinder way to meet the week you actually have.

  • You feel unlike yourself and cannot name why
  • Worry, sadness, or numbness is taking up the day
  • You want a private place that does not require you to be fine
Request individual therapy
02 — Couples

Couples Counseling

Strengthen your relationship

For partners who want to speak and hear each other more clearly. We slow the argument down, name the pattern underneath it, and practice a different way of reaching.

This is not about deciding a winner. It is about making the relationship a place both people can tell the truth.

  • The same fight keeps returning in a new costume
  • Distance, resentment, or silence has settled between you
  • You want to repair something before it hardens

If there is ongoing violence or control in the relationship, couples therapy is not the right starting place. Reach out anyway, and we can help you think about a safer next step.

Request couples counseling
03 — Family

Family Support

Grow together

For parents, caregivers, and families figuring out how to show up for one another through change. The aim is a room where more than one voice can be heard without someone having to disappear.

If a child or teen would be part of the work, we talk first about whether this practice is the right fit for them. That conversation happens before anyone is asked to sit down together.

  • Home has become a place of walking on eggshells
  • A change — a move, a loss, a new shape of family — needs company
  • You want help speaking across generations
Request family support
04 — Transitions

Life Transitions

Embrace what's next

Thresholds deserve company. A move, a loss, a career change, becoming a parent, ending a relationship, retirement, or a shift in identity can leave you both grateful and unsteady.

We make room for the part of you that is ready and the part that is not. Growing, here, is allowed to include grief.

  • A chapter ended and the next one has not started
  • Everyone expects you to be excited, and you are also afraid
  • You want help choosing a pace that does not abandon you
Request support for a transition

We can decide together.

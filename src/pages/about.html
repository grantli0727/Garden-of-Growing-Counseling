About the practice

A practice rooted in care.

Garden of Growing Counseling is a quiet place to set things down, tell the truth, and find a way forward that still feels like you.

Why this room exists

Healing does not have to be loud to be real.

People come here carrying anxiety, grief, relationship strain, family patterns, and the quieter sense that life should feel different than it does.

You do not need a crisis that looks a certain way. You do not need the right words. The work is unhurried. Sessions are a place to tell the truth, notice what you already carry, and practice something kinder than the pace you have been keeping.

If meeting in person is not possible, video sessions are held with the same care. The point is a reliable hour that belongs to you.

How we practice

Four things you can count on

I

Presence

The hour belongs to you. We stay with what is actually here, not with a script for who you should be by the end of it.

II

Respect

You are the expert on your life. Nothing in the room requires you to perform being well, grateful, or easy to help.

III

Patience

Change is allowed to be slow. A story that took years to form does not have to resolve on a schedule.

IV

Practical care

Insight matters when it can live in an ordinary Tuesday. We often leave with one small thing to try, not a stack of assignments.

A good fit

Talk therapy, held simply.

The work is person-centered and practical.

We pay attention to the story you tell and to the way your body keeps score of it. We look for strengths that are already growing, even when they are easy to miss.

Before we begin ongoing sessions, we talk about fees, scheduling, and whether this is the right room. You are not committing to a long course of care by reaching out.

If you are in immediate danger, this site is not the right door. Call or text 988, or call 911.

About the practice

A safe space for your next chapter

At Garden of Growing Counseling, we believe in creating a warm, supportive environment where you can explore your thoughts and feelings without judgment.

Together, we'll work toward meaningful change, at a pace that feels right for you. You do not need a crisis that looks a certain way, and you do not need the right words.

Our approach

Care that grows at your pace

I

Presence

The hour belongs to you. We stay with what is actually here, not with a script for who you should be by the end of it.

II

Patience

Change is allowed to be slow. A story that took years to form does not have to resolve on a schedule.

III

Practical care

Insight matters when it can live in an ordinary Tuesday. We often leave with one small thing to try, not a stack of assignments.

Most of a garden's life is root, weather, and waiting. Growth is allowed to be quiet and still be real.
